Using the logo

A few simple rules keep the brand looking right.

Give it room

Leave clear space around the logo at least equal to the width of the vertical bar in the mark.

Keep the colours

Do not recolour, outline, stretch or rotate it. Pick the primary or reversed version for contrast.

Minimum size

Wordmark: 120 px wide on screen. Mark alone: 16 px. Below that, use the mark.

One accent at a time

Amber is a small highlight, never a background for body text.
