Questions we hear a lot
Cannot find your answer? Message us from your Ladybug dashboard and we reply.

    

      
Do I need to pay to try Ladybug API?
No. You get a free test key with 50 requests, no signup. The live panel at the top of this page uses the same key.

      
How does Ladybug Cloud pricing work?
There is a free 7 day trial with no card. After that you can run one bot for 24 hours on 20 free coins a day, or go unlimited for $3 a month with no coins needed.

      
What is Ladybug Mini?
It is our ready-made WhatsApp bot with 42+ commands for group admin, downloads, AI and stickers. Ladybug Cloud keeps it online.

      
Which currencies do you accept?
Everything we sell is priced in US dollars or ZiG.

      
Can I manage bots for my own clients?
Yes. Resellers set up and manage several bots for clients from one dashboard.

      
What can you modify?
Apps, games, websites, bots and tools, plus integrations between them. Describe the goal, get a scope and price, then we build it, test it and hand it over with install notes.

      
How do I join Ntandomods?
Developers can join for $10. Choose "Join Ntandomods as a developer" in the contact section, or message us through your Ladybug account, and we will take it from there.

      
Do you provide domains and DNS?
Yes. Tell us the domain or subdomain you want and where it should point, and we set up the DNS records. You can check them any time with the DNS lookup tool.

      
Is there a status page?
Yes. The platform health badge in the footer of this page shows whether Ladybug API is up right now, and the Ladybug site has a public status page.

      
Where can I get your logo and colours?
On the brand kit page, with logos in SVG and PNG, colour codes, fonts, social covers and a print-ready business card.
